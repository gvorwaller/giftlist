# 2026-09-16 Devlog

## td-665d01 — Amazon shipment reconciliation and existing-gift review

Implemented locally following approval of the September 14 investigation and the explicit requirement to choose a named person and existing gift when marking a shipment.

### Problem and behavior

Amazon combined the birthday cards and household gloves from two orders into one notification. The old parser retained only the first order reference; the order of those references changed in the delivery email. Shipment acceptance could therefore create a duplicate under the other order. The purchase matcher also excluded gifts already carrying an order number, and one unmatched household item prevented known siblings from advancing.

Shipment and delivery emails now use a separate per-item reconciliation path. A unique exact normalized description plus any validated order reference proposes the existing gift, including legacy gifts with `order_id` but no `order_pk`. Matching does not depend on an AI response. It preserves the gift's recipient, occasion, original order and line index. Archived gifts remain duplicate evidence, and repeated titles or quantities require an explicit choice. Order-confirmation behavior and manual lifecycle buttons remain separate.

Review shows “Mark [person]’s [gift] as shipped/delivered,” or a person filter and existing-gift picker. Each item can be left pending, ignored, explicitly linked, or explicitly created. Multiple units require distinct gift selections, including gifts for different recipients. Explicit creation requires selecting the source order when references exist. Terminal statuses are preserved.

Delivery can advance an ordered gift directly to delivered. Timestamps use the notification's received time as the available event evidence, not commit time. A delivery notification alone does not invent a shipping date. A late shipment notification can fill that missing timestamp without moving status backwards.

### Persistence and safeguards

- Migration 029 adds the complete email order-reference list, durable per-item decisions, and item-to-gift/shipment links. It does not rewrite existing gifts or infer missing references for old imports.
- Preview and commit share the same planner. Commit re-reads and validates inside a short SQLite transaction. Changed suggestions, unavailable/private gifts, repeated use of one gift, incomplete quantity selections and invalid order choices are rejected atomically.
- Partial commits retain resolved items and leave the email pending until every item is resolved. Repeated submissions do not create or advance gifts twice. Ignore/skip decisions are recorded.
- One email can link shipments under multiple orders through the shared source message ID. Shipment contents come from durable item links plus pre-existing shipment assignments, not the email's first order.
- The high-confidence action uses deterministic shipment evidence and requires every item to be resolvable. AI purchase verdicts cannot bypass shipment review. Shipment scans no longer make purchase-matcher calls.
- The gift catalog is shared across review rows and respects self-person visibility; there is no arbitrary candidate cap.
